# Repository contribution guidance

## Terminology and evidence conventions

Follow [TERMINOLOGY.md](TERMINOLOGY.md) for new and edited material. Lead with the
recognized mechanism and precise function; qualify historical aliases by source.
Preserve stable interface identifiers and frozen evidence. Use producer-owned
types at adapter boundaries and test meaning-preserving field translations.
Keep implementation, proof, empirical evidence and verification freshness
separate. These conventions supplement existing authority and review rules.
